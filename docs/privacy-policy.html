Foundations Practice Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Foundations Practice app for Android ("the app") handles your information. The app is an independent study tool developed by diazjet123. It is not affiliated with Anthropic.

Information the app collects

The app does not collect, send, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own, and it does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the result of your most recent practice session (score, number of correct answers, topic, and completion time) on your device so it can show it on the home screen. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Links to other websites

Answer explanations link to public documentation. Opening a link takes you to that website in your browser, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
