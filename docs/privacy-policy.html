Foundations Practice Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how the Foundations Practice app for Android ("the app") handles your information. The app is an independent study tool developed by diazjet123. It is not affiliated with Anthropic.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no server of its own. Some versions show ads and offer a one-time purchase to remove them; the sections below describe what that involves. If your version of the app shows no ads, the advertising section does not apply to you.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, including your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This information is collected and handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Google explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads, and ads are only requested if your choices allow it. You can change your choices at any time with the Privacy choices button on the app's home screen.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (on most Android devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

Purchases

You can pay once to remove ads. The purchase is processed by Google Play, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. The app never sees your payment details. It asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase so it can turn ads off, including after you reinstall the app or move to a new device. After a purchase the app stops loading ads and no longer initializes the advertising SDK.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the result of your most recent practice session (score, number of correct answers, topic, and completion time) and whether you have removed ads on your device. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app; a purchase is restored from Google Play the next time you open the app.

Links to other websites

Answer explanations link to public documentation. Opening a link takes you to that website in your browser, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
